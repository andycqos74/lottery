## The problem

Most small society lotteries run on goodwill and a spreadsheet. One volunteer
keeps the member list. Someone else checks bank statements line by line to see
who paid. The draw happens when someone remembers to run it. If that person is
ill, on holiday or steps down, the whole thing is at risk.

It works until it doesn't. Then it costs you members, money and trust.

---

## What it does

### Draws that run themselves
Set up a single draw or a full weekly, fortnightly or monthly series in one go.
Entries close at the cutoff you set, and the draw runs at the advertised time
without anyone pressing a button. If a draw hasn't finished when it should
have, your team is told.

### Fair numbers you can prove
Winning numbers come from RANDOM.ORG, an independent source of true randomness,
not from a script on someone's laptop. Every result is recorded and can't be
changed afterwards.

### Members play the way they want
Members log in, pick their numbers and pay by card or Direct Debit. Their
numbers stay in every draw until they change them. If they buy again with the
same numbers, the weeks add up. If they pick new numbers, they get an extra
line. They're told what happened on screen and by email.

### Existing standing orders keep working
Members don't have to sign up again. Upload your bank's transaction export and
the system matches payments to members for you. Anything it isn't sure about
goes to a volunteer to check, so it never guesses.

### Paper tickets and agents included
Sell tickets in the clubhouse or through local agents? Your staff can enter a
paper ticket in seconds, using the player's own numbers or a quick pick, and
it's entered into the right draws automatically.

### Jackpots handled properly
An unwon jackpot rolls over to the next draw. A must-be-won draw rolls down to
the best match-3, then match-2, then match-1 winners. Shared prizes are split
fairly, and any leftover pennies go to your good cause, never lost to rounding.

### Every penny accounted for
Each £2 is split between prize fund, good cause and running costs and recorded
in a ledger that can only be added to, never edited. When the treasurer,
auditor or licensing authority asks, the answer is already there.

### Nothing gets lost
When something needs a person, like a bank payment nobody can identify or a
decision only your committee can make, the system opens a task and waits. Open
tasks are flagged more prominently the longer they go unanswered.

- **It never invents a rule.** If your scheme hasn't decided something, the
  system stops and asks a person.
- **Two people for big decisions.** Sensitive actions need two different
  approvers.
- **Your data stays private.** Member details are encrypted, and the platform
  is hosted on infrastructure you control.
